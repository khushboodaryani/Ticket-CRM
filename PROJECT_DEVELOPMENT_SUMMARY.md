# Ticket CRM: Project Development Summary

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the features and modules developed for the Ticket CRM platform. It is designed to be easily understood by non-technical stakeholders.

---

## 🌟 Platform Overview
The Ticket CRM is a professional support platform designed to manage customer requests efficiently. It ensures that every issue is tracked, prioritized, and resolved according to agreed-upon timelines (SLAs), while providing real-time insights for management.

---

## 🛠️ Key Feature Modules

### 1. Smart Ticket Management
*   **Centralized Ticket Flow**: Easily create and track support tickets from one place.
*   **Ticket States**: Track tickets through various stages—from "New" to "Resolved"—with clear status updates.
*   **Detailed Records**: Every ticket maintains a history of comments, status changes, and assigned owners.

### 2. SLA Engine (Timed Resolution)
*   **Priority Levels**: Tickets are automatically categorized from P1 (Critical) to P5 (Low).
*   **Automated Tracking**: The system calculates how much time is left to resolve an issue based on business hours.
*   **Escalation Alerts**: If a ticket isn't resolved on time, it is automatically flagged or escalated to managers.

### 3. User & Team Organization
*   **Role-Based Access**: Specialized views for Agents, Team Leaders, Managers, and Administrators—ensuring everyone sees only what they need.
*   **Team Management**: Ability to group users into professional teams for better workload distribution.
*   **Shift & Holiday Planning**: Integrated tools to manage staff shifts and public holidays, ensuring the SLA engine knows when the team is working.

### 4. Lead & Customer Management
*   **Customer Directory**: Maintain a clean list of all clients and their contact details.
*   **Lead Tracking**: Manage potential sales or interest-based "leads" through a structured workflow.
*   **Bulk Data Import**: A powerful tool to upload thousands of records at once via CSV files, with automatic checks for duplicates and errors.

### 5. Automated Communications
*   **Email Notifications**: Automatic alerts sent to customers and agents when tickets are created or updated.
*   **System Notifications**: In-app alerts to keep staff updated on urgent tasks.

### 6. Executive Dashboard
*   **Real-Time Analytics**: A visual overview of team performance, ticket volumes, and resolution success rates.
*   **Status Charts**: Interactive graphs showing the current health of the support queue.
